LIFE & CONTRIBUTIONS

A Journey of Unyielding Dedication

🌊

1. Early Life and Humble Beginnings

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, to a boat owner and homemaker, young Kalam delivered newspapers before school hours to support his family's modest finances. Driven by an insatiable curiosity about flight and avian physics, he graduated in physics from St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, and went on to study aerospace engineering at the prestigious Madras Institute of Technology (MIT).

Though narrowly missing his childhood dream of becoming an Indian Air Force fighter pilot, Kalam redirected his passion toward indigenous defense research, joining the Aeronautical Development Establishment of the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO).

🚀

2. Architect of India's Space & Missile Capabilities

In 1969, Kalam transferred to the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), serving as the Project Director of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III). In July 1980, the SLV-III successfully deployed the Rohini satellite into near-Earth orbit, firmly establishing India as an elite global space-faring power.

Returning to DRDO in 1982, Dr. Kalam spearheaded the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP). Under his visionary guidance, India successfully developed strategic defense missile systems including Agni (intermediate-range ballistic missile) and Prithvi (surface-to-surface missile), earning him the enduring title of "The Missile Man of India."

🏛️

3. The People's President & National Vision 2020

In 2002, Dr. Kalam was elected the 11th President of the Republic of India with overwhelming cross-party support. During his tenure at Rashtrapati Bhavan, he opened the presidential gates to ordinary citizens, students, and grassroots innovators, rightfully earning the moniker of the "People's President."

He articulated India Millennium Missions (Vision 2020)—a master plan advocating for economic self-reliance, rural connectivity (PURA), world-class technological infrastructure, and the empowerment of youth through science, education, and moral integrity.

📚

4. The Dedicated Teacher Until the Final Breath

After completing his presidential term, Dr. Kalam returned with immense joy to his true calling: teaching and engaging with the youth. He took on visiting professorships at IIM Ahmedabad, IIM Shillong, and IITs across India, personally interacting with over 15 million students across two decades.

On 27 July 2015, while delivering a lecture on "Creating a Livable Planet Earth" at IIM Shillong, Dr. Kalam collapsed and passed away doing what he loved most—educating and inspiring the next generation.
