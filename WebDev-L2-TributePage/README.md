# Task 2: Tribute Page — Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

**Track:** Web Development & Designing  
**Level:** Level 2  
**Project Name:** `WebDev-L2-TributePage`  
**Internship:** Oasis Infobyte SIP

---

## 🌟 Overview
A dedicated, responsive biographical tribute website celebrating the life, scientific breakthroughs, leadership, and immortal legacy of **Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam** (1931–2015) — the "Missile Man of India" and 11th President of India.

---

## ✨ Features Checklist
- [x] **Header & Subject Identity:** Title with subject name, lifespan, prominent taglines, and an authentic vector portrait card.
- [x] **Extensive Biographical Narrative:** 4 structured chapters of comprehensive original content covering Early Life & Education, Space & Missile Development (ISRO & DRDO), Presidency (Vision 2020), and Passionate Teaching.
- [x] **Chronological Timeline:** Interactive vertical timeline documenting key life milestones from 1931 to 2015.
- [x] **Distinct Quote Blocks:** Notable philosophical and motivational quotes styled with Playfair Display serif typography and gold accents.
- [x] **Multiple Background Shades:** Seamless transitions between Deep Navy, Rich Slate, Charcoal, and Gold-accented sections.
- [x] **Dual Typography Pairing:** Elegant serif headings (`Cinzel` & `Playfair Display`) paired with readable modern sans-serif body copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).
- [x] **Full Responsiveness:** Mobile-optimised layout with flexible timeline adaptation.
